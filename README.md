## What Doesn't Work (Post-Mainnet)

- **Waiting for the old pre-deposit** — Season 1's deposit-and-hold mechanic is closed. Idle margin does not earn AURA the way pre-deposits once did; trading volume, BulkSOL, and referrals do.
- **Farming with zero volume** — mainnet AURA tracks activity. A funded account that never trades earns far less than an active one.
- **Sybil farming** — BULK has on-chain identity signals. One wallet with genuine activity beats split exposure.

---

## AURA Notes — What to Know

Pre-mainnet (Season 1), 1,000,000 AURA was split weekly across pre-depositors by USDC × time held. That program is closed. On mainnet, the per-volume trading formula has not been published — the team will publish full rules before TGE. The direction is confirmed by kdot: early real funds and mainnet trading both earn more allocation.

---

## Risk Disclosure

Trading margin at BULK is Solana USDC — withdrawable at any time. Smart contract risk exists (Zellic audit confirmed August 17, 2026; upgrades gated by a 5-of-8 Squads multisig). The AURA-to-BULK conversion rate at TGE has not been published. 30% community allocation is confirmed; distribution details are not fully documented. BulkSOL is an early-mainnet LST. The loop strategy adds liquidation risk.

Not financial advice. Do not use capital you cannot afford to lose.
